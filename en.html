Privacy Policy — Boothisfactor

Last updated: 17 November 2026
App version: 2.0.0 and later
Owner: [NAME / COMPANY]
Contact: boothisfactor@gmail.com

In short: Boothisfactor works fully offline. It has no Internet access, needs no account, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device. The owner never receives and cannot see any of your data.

1. Who we are and what the app does

Boothisfactor ("the App") manages inventory and sales at fair and event booths. This policy explains how the App handles data, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR").

2. Data the App keeps on your device

The following data is stored in a local database, in the App's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to the owner or to third parties: the App does not have the network permission.

CategoryExamples
InventoryProduct names, codes, types, categories, sizes, prices, costs and stock; product images you pick; stores and currencies.
SalesDate and time, items, quantities, discounts, payment method (a label only, no payment data), free-text notes, currency.
EventsNames, codes, dates and costs (participation, transport, accommodation, variable).
Operator nameThe name you enter at first launch, used for the greeting and in report headers.
SettingsOffers, theme, language, shop logo, automatic backup preferences.

Free-text fields. Sale notes are free text. If you enter other people's personal data there (for example a customer's name), you are responsible for it as the controller.

3. Device permissions

  • Photos / files: requested only when you pick an image for a product or the logo, or a file to import or restore. The App reads only the file you choose.
  • No Internet access: the published App does not request the network permission.

4. Backups, exports and transfer

  • Backups and exports (.bfb, PDF, TSV/CSV files) are created only when you ask, or automatically if you turn on automatic backup. They contain your sales and inventory data unencrypted. You decide where to save them and who to share them with (for example by e-mail or a cloud service of your choice); from then on that service's terms apply.
  • Android/Google cloud backup: disabled. The database and the in-app copies are excluded.
  • Phone-to-phone transfer: when you use the system feature to copy data to a new device, the App's data moves directly between the two phones (cable or local network), never through the owner.

5. Legal basis and retention

The owner does not process personal data on its own behalf: it neither receives nor stores it. Data stays on your device until you delete it in the App, uninstall the App, or clear its data in Android settings. Uninstalling removes the database and in-app copies, but not backup or export files you saved elsewhere.

6. Third parties

The App includes no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs. Installation and updates happen through Google Play (or the APK file, if you install it manually), which processes its own data under its own policy.

7. Children

The App is meant for business operators and is not directed at children under 14.

8. Your rights

Since all data is under your control, you can view, export, correct or delete it directly in the App. For any question, write to boothisfactor@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: www.garanteprivacy.it).

9. Changes

If a future version adds online features (for example accounts, sync or in-app purchases), this policy will be updated before release and the date above will change.
